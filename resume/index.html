Projects

  • 2025
    Active Shadowing (ASD) for Implicit HRI Communication
    • Contributed to the development of Active Shadowing, a novel system using Augmented Reality shadows to enhance robot collaboration without sacrificing motion optimality.
    • Developed the core robot control software, precisely choreographing a UR5 arm's movements to seamlessly integrate with a predictive Augmented Reality shadow projected via a HoloLens.
    • Co-designed the 23-participant user study that validated the system, proving via t-test (p<0.05) that the AR shadow improved human-robot safety scores by over 50% compared to explicit communication.
  • 2025
    LLM as a Model Optimizer in Model Predictive Control
    • Proved that an LLM can function as a real-time model optimizer for an MPC system, successfully adapting the controller parameters online to stabilize a dynamic inverted pendulum simulation.
    • Engineered a physical testbed for hardware validation by designing and 3D-printing a custom pendulum and integrating it with a UR5 robotic arm.
  • 2020
    Office Reception Robot
    • Engineered the robot's perception and interaction systems, integrating a Python-based computer vision module for facial recognition and Google Dialogflow for natural language understanding (NLU).
    • Built the full-stack architecture, including a Python back-end and 3 custom Android front-ends, to successfully automate 3 core office tasks (guest registration, package delivery, and meeting scheduling).
  • 2017
    Assistive Social Robot (Capstone Project)
    • Designed, built, and deployed a social robot from the ground up, developing the full stack using Unity (C#), Android (Java), and Arduino (C++).
    • The project's success and technical novelty directly led to securing a $26,000 grant from Pakistan's Higher Education Commission (TDF-02145), which funded its continuation as a formal research position.
