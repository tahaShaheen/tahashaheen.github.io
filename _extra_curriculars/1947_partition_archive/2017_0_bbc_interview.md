In 2017, I was interviewed by BBC Radio as part of a documentary titled "Pakistan, Partition And The Present". I spoke of my personal journey of recording Partition eyewitness accounts and why the work with the [1947 Partition Archive](https://www.1947partitionarchive.org/) was important to me. 

Intervieweing team was composed of [Shahzeb Jillani](https://www.linkedin.com/in/shahzeb-jillani-2539313/), a BBC correspondent in Pakistan, and [Tim Mansel](https://www.linkedin.com/in/timmansel/), a Producer for BBC World Service.
