[ISA](https://www.isa.co.jp/english/about/) is a Japanese educational and cultural exchange company that offers a variety of [programs](https://www.isa.co.jp/english/service/). As a group leader for the Empowerment Program, I assisted a facilitator and lead groups of Japanese high school students in intensive programs designed to improve their English speaking, presentation, and discussion skills. Topics included the United Nation’s Sustainable Development Goals, positive thinking, technology, personal identity, diversity, and critical thinking. This position allowed me to travel to many places in Japan and meet many extra ordinary Middle School and High School students.
