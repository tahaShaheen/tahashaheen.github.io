I collaborated with a team of four undergraduate students and therapists from [Dow Institute of Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation](https://www.duhs.edu.pk/new/ipmr-introduction/) to augment a [Robosapien by WowWee robotics](https://wowwee.com/robosapien-x). We used [OpenPose](https://github.com/CMU-Perceptual-Computing-Lab/openpose) and an [ESP8266 nodemcu](https://www.nodemcu.com/) to enable the robot to (1) mimic a single person's arm movements and (2) recognize when a person had successfully imitated the robot's arm position.
